### **3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?**  
They look for self‑awareness, honesty, and how you handle improvement.

---

### **4. Why are you leaving your current job (or why did you leave your last job)?**  
They want to ensure you’re not leaving due to performance or conflict issues.

---

### **5. Where do you see yourself in the next 3–5 years?**  
They check your ambition and whether you plan to stay long enough.

---

### **6. Tell me about a challenge you faced at work and how you handled it.**  
This reveals your problem‑solving, communication, and ownership.

---

### **7. Describe a situation where you worked in a team.**  
They want to see collaboration, leadership, and conflict‑resolution skills.

---

### **8. What motivates you?**  
They want to understand what drives your performance — learning, impact, recognition, growth, etc.

---

### **9. Why should we hire you?**  
This is your chance to sell your strengths and show how you fit the role.

---

### **10. Do you have any questions for us?**  
This is a test of your seriousness and curiosity. Never say “No questions”.
