Nexcenter is NTT’s global brand for enterprise-grade data centers, offering highly reliable, disaster-resistant facilities across Japan and APAC. These centers are designed for business continuity, with redundant power, cooling, and network systems, and are widely used by enterprises needing secure, scalable IT infrastructure. 

🌐 What Is Nexcenter?
Brand: Nexcenter is NTT’s flagship data center service brand.
Scope: Operates across Japan and Asia-Pacific, integrated into NTT’s Global Data Center (GDC) network.
Purpose: Provides secure, high-availability environments for enterprise IT workloads, cloud platforms, and digital transformation initiatives.

🏢 Key Features of Nexcenter Data Centers
Business Continuity: Facilities are located in areas with minimal risk of earthquakes, floods, or other natural disasters.
High Availability: Redundant electricity, cooling, and network systems minimize downtime.
Security & Compliance: Certified to international standards (ISO, SOC, etc.), with strong physical and cyber security.
Scalability: Supports enterprise expansion across APAC with standardized infrastructure.
Integration: Connects seamlessly with NTT’s Digital Fabric and global network backbone.

📊 Nexcenter vs. Typical Data Centers
Feature | Nexcenter (NTT) | Typical Data Center
-----------------------------------------------
Disaster Resistance | Built in low-risk zones, redundant systems | Varies by provider
Global Reach | Integrated with NTT’s worldwide GDC network | Often regional only
Security | Top-level certifications, biometrics, access logs | May lack uniform standards
Integration | Direct links to NTT cloud, network, and partner ecosystems | Limited interconnectivity
Reliability | Industry-leading uptime and stability | Depends on operator

📍 Regions & Clients
Japan (JP): Operated by NTT Communications, serving domestic enterprises.
APAC: Operated by NTT Limited, supporting multinational corporations.
Clients: Enterprises needing mission-critical hosting, cloud providers, and industries like finance, telecom, and manufacturing.

⚠️ Considerations
Premium pricing: Nexcenter is positioned for enterprise workloads, not small-scale hosting.
Vendor lock-in: Deep integration with NTT’s ecosystem may make migration complex.
Best fit: Large enterprises needing global reach, compliance, and resilience.
